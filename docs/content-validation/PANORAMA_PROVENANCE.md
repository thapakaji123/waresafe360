## Prompt record

Shared prompt constraints: “Create a seamless full 360-degree equirectangular warehouse safety-training panorama, exact 2:1 aspect ratio, realistic professional industrial environment, eye-level central viewpoint, coherent wraparound geometry and lighting, no text overlays, no hotspot markers, no logos, no gore. Stage clearly visible but plausible safety hazards around the full sphere for an educational prototype.”

Scene variations requested: a low-risk induction/tutorial area; a busy loading bay with dock and vehicle hazards; tall storage aisles with racking/stacking/housekeeping hazards; a shared forklift and pedestrian zone with traffic-separation hazards; packing/dispatch workstations with ergonomic and conveyor hazards; and an emergency/fire area with evacuation, extinguisher, spill, and electrical hazards.

## Approval status

The files pass automated existence and aspect-ratio checks. Hotspot coordinates are prototype placements and must be visually checked in the target browser. The generated scenes may contain visual ambiguity or unrealistic details. A qualified workplace-safety reviewer and the client must approve each depicted hazard, coordinate, question, correct answer, explanation, and site-specific instruction before operational use.
